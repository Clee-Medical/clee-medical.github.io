# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2025**

This Privacy Policy explains how Clee Medical SA ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit our website www.cleemedical.com (the "Website") or submit information through our online questionnaire. We are committed to complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable US privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

## 1. Who We Are

Clee Medical SA is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at:

- **Email:** [contactus@cleemedical.com](mailto:contactus@cleemedical.com)
- **Website:** [www.cleemedical.com](https://www.cleemedical.com)

## 2. Data We Collect

**Via the Website (automatically):**

- Usage data such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and device/browser information, collected through analytics tools (Microsoft Clarity).

**Via the online questionnaire (voluntarily provided):**

- Responses you submit through our questionnaire, hosted by Tally.so.
- Any contact details you choose to provide (e.g., name, email address) if you request follow-up from us.

We apply data minimization: we only ask for information that is necessary for the purposes described below. We do not knowingly collect health-related personal data through this Website.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data to:

- Understand user needs and preferences in order to improve our products and Website (questionnaire responses).
- Analyze Website usage to improve site performance and content (analytics).
- Respond to your requests when you contact us.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

- **Consent:** You provide explicit consent when submitting the questionnaire. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, without affecting prior processing.
- **Legitimate interests:** We use analytics tools to improve our Website, based on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used.
- **Legal obligations:** We may retain or disclose data where required by law.

## 5. Cookies and Analytics

Our Website uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics purposes via Microsoft Clarity. Clarity may record anonymized session data (clicks, scrolls, and interactions) to help us improve the Website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so will not prevent you from using the Website.

## 6. Data Processors and Transfers

We use the following third-party service providers ("processors") who may process data on our behalf:

- **Tally.so (Tally Labs NV, Belgium):** hosts and processes our questionnaire responses.
- **Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation, USA):** provides Website analytics.

Data may be transferred to, and stored in, countries outside the EU/EEA (including the United States). Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 7. Data Retention

Questionnaire responses are retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which they were collected, and in line with our data minimization principles, after which they are deleted or anonymized. Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of the respective provider.

## 8. Your Rights

**If you are in the EU/EEA (GDPR), you have the right to:**

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Rectify inaccurate personal data.
- Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Withdraw consent at any time.
- Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Switzerland: the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner; in the EU: your national data protection authority).

**If you are a US resident (CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws), you have the right to:**

- Know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
- Request deletion of your personal information.
- Request access to your personal information.
- Opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information for monetary compensation, but analytics usage may be considered "sharing" under some state laws; you may opt out by contacting us or disabling cookies.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [contactus@cleemedical.com](mailto:contactus@cleemedical.com). We will respond within 30 days. Please note that we may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

## 9. Children's Privacy

This Website is not directed at children under 13 (US) or under 16 (EU) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above indicates the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

## 12. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at [contactus@cleemedical.com](mailto:contactus@cleemedical.com).
